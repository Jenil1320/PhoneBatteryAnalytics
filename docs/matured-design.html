What's new vs today's app: a three-source SoH that shows measured / modeled / gauge side by side for confidence; a degradation-model screen that explains the fade as calendar vs cyclic aging from the phone's own logged temperature and charge exposure; real endurance estimates ("lasts for" at three usage levels); and full session logs with efficiency and estimated per-app drain.

The simSES angle: the modeled SoH runs a semi-empirical aging model — calendar fade scaling with √time, an Arrhenius temperature factor and a charge-level stress factor, plus cyclic fade from depth-of-discharge and cycle throughput — parameterised by the exposure data the Pixel already records (swelling_data = hours at each temperature/charge level, and the cycle-count distribution). It's the household-storage degradation approach, pointed at a phone cell.

Build order: this mockup → Compose implementation of Health (2a) + model (2b) → Now/endurance (2c) → History/sessions (2d). Nav shown as Health · Now · History · Apps; the current Analytics/Drains content folds into History and Apps.
